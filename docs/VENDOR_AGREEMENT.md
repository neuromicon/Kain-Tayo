## Vendor Commitments

1. **BUY 1 + GIVE 1 Mechanism:**  
   The vendor offers BUY 1 + GIVE 1 at the agreed normal price of ₱_______ per standard rice serving. The customer pays for two servings, receives one immediately, and funds one prepaid serving.

2. **Full Upfront Payment:**  
   The vendor receives full payment for both servings at the time of purchase. No donation-processing fee or commission is deducted.

3. **Rice Bank Credit:**  
   For every paid BUY 1 + GIVE 1 transaction, the vendor records exactly one prepaid serving in its own Rice Bank.

4. **Honor Commitment:**  
   The vendor agrees to honor every outstanding prepaid serving when claimed. The recipient is not charged.

5. **Same-Vendor Redemption:**  
   In Version 1, credits created at this vendor may be claimed only at this same vendor.

6. **Daily Accounting:**  
   The vendor maintains daily opening balance, new prepaid servings, redemptions, closing balance, and keeps a paper counter sheet.

7. **Portion & Quality:**  
   The vendor serves one standard portion per person per visit, prepared fresh when requested, for immediate consumption.

8. **Food Safety & Compliance:**  
   The vendor remains responsible for food preparation, sanitary permits, and handler health certificates.

9. **Dignity & Zero Stigma:**  
   The vendor displays approved signage and does **not** require beneficiary ID, income proof, registration, or personal explanations.

10. **Privacy Protection:**  
    The vendor will not photograph, publicly identify, or disclose information about people claiming free servings without informed consent.

11. **Withdrawal Notice:**  
    If leaving the program, the vendor stops accepting new credits and agrees on a plan to honor all remaining outstanding servings.
