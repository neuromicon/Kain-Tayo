# Start Kain Tayo / Rice Circle in Your City

A practical, community-led launch guide for replicating the **BUY 1 + GIVE 1** model in your municipality.

---

## The 6 Launch Steps

### 1. Find Local Food Partners
Talk to 3–5 trusted local carinderias, small eateries, or university-area food stalls. Explain the concept:
- Customer pays for 2 cups, receives 1.
- Vendor receives full payment for both servings upfront.
- Vendor commits to serve the extra cup when someone asks.
- Agree on standard portion size and fair price (typically ₱10–₱15).

### 2. Agree on Simple Rules
- Keep it simple: no IDs, no paperwork, no questions asked.
- 1 standard warm serving per person per visit for immediate consumption.
- Priority for students, schoolchildren, and vulnerable community members.
- Credits created at a vendor stay redeemable at that same vendor (Version 1 model).

### 3. Provide Counter Materials
Print the materials from [`/public/assets/aistudio/Kain_Tayo_Printable_Toolkit_654d1884.pdf`](../public/assets/aistudio/Kain_Tayo_Printable_Toolkit_654d1884.pdf):
- Display the public wall poster.
- Place the counter table tent sign where customers order.
- Provide paper tally sheets or a counter chalkboard.

### 4. Food Safety & Sanitary Compliance
- Partner only with vendors holding valid municipal sanitary permits and health certificates.
- Rice must be kept hot under local health standards.
- Maintain coordination with the local City Health Office.

### 5. Invite the Community
- Engage university student councils, local youth groups, and neighborhood businesses.
- Encourage alumni and civic clubs to sponsor servings.
- Spread respectful Bisaya / Tagalog / English phrasing so no one feels shame when asking.

### 6. Review & Iterate
- Conduct weekly check-ins with cooks.
- Ensure vendors feel supported and payments are honored seamlessly.
- Address any shortages or discrepancies.
