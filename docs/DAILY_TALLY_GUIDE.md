# Daily Counter Tally Guide

How partner carinderias record and reconcile prepaid rice servings.

---

## The Daily Formula

$$\text{Opening Balance} + \text{Prepaid Servings Added} - \text{Servings Redeemed} = \text{Closing Balance}$$

---

## Procedure for Counter Staff

1. **Morning Start (Opening Balance):**  
   Check the closing balance from the previous day. Write this number at the top of today's tally sheet.

2. **When a Customer Pays for Extra Rice:**  
   - Accept payment for 2 servings (e.g. ₱24).
   - Hand the customer their meal.
   - On the tally sheet, record time, quantity (`+1` or `+2`), and update the running balance.

3. **When Someone Asks for a Rice Circle Serving:**  
   - Greet them warmly with a smile.
   - Scoop one standard portion of hot rice onto a plate or takeaway container.
   - On the tally sheet, record time and mark `1 redeemed` (`-1`).
   - Do **not** ask for ID, name, or student proof.

4. **Closing (Evening):**  
   - Count total added and total redeemed.
   - Verify that: `Opening + Added - Redeemed = Closing`.
   - Keep the sheet in the counter folder for spot-checks.
